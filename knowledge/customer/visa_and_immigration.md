# Visa & immigration — customer FAQ (GGBH)

**Audience:** prospective customers with visa/immigration doubts (their own visa, a partner's, or an
employee's). Answer in plain language; never guess at a customer's specific eligibility — confirm
the general rule here, and flag anything genuinely case-specific to a GGBH consultant.

## Do I even need a UAE residence visa?

**No, if you hold GCC nationality** — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, or Bahrain. GCC
nationals don't need a UAE residence visa at all; there's a separate, simpler path (work permit +
Emirates ID, or the family book for UAE nationals). If this applies to you, tell your consultant so
they can skip the visa process and set you up the right way.

Everyone else generally needs a residence visa to live and work in the UAE, sponsored either by
their company (Establishment Card) or a family member.

## Age requirements

- **Employment visa:** applicant must be between 18 and 65 years old.
- **Partner / Investor visa:** applicant must be at least 18 years old.

## Investor vs. Partner visa — what's the difference?

- **Investor** designation generally applies to a shareholder in the company.
- **Partner** designation requires your **share capital to be AED 48,000 or more**. Below that
  threshold, you would not qualify as a Partner — you may still qualify as an Investor or under an
  employee designation instead, depending on your role.
- Either way, whether you qualify as Investor or Partner is ultimately confirmed against your
  company's Memorandum/Articles of Association (AOA), not just your own preference on a form.
- **Salary cap:** if you're on an Investor or Partner visa, your declared salary should not exceed
  **AED 15,000**. Above that, a different visa category applies.

## What documents will I need?

This varies by visa type and nationality (see `customer/nationality_document_requirements.md` for
nationality-specific extras), but in general:

- Passport copy (and sometimes specific pages — confirmed per nationality).
- A recent photo (specifications shared during processing).
- For Investor/Partner applications, a **personal bank statement**:
  - Covering the most recent **3 months** (some nationalities require 6 months — confirmed by your
    consultant).
  - Showing a balance of **AED 50,000 or more** that doesn't drop below that in any of the months
    covered.
  - A **detailed transaction statement**, not just a summary of opening/closing balances — your
    bank can issue this on request.
  - Your full name and account details must be visible on the statement.
- Health insurance (see below).

## Health insurance

Health insurance is **mandatory for every visa holder**, including Investor/Partner applicants. It
must be issued by a UAE-licensed insurer; basic coverage is acceptable. GGBH can help arrange this as
part of your application.

Employees also generally receive **Employee Protection Insurance (EPI)** as part of new employment,
transfer, renewal, or work-permit processing — this does not apply to company shareholders (even if
they're on an employee-type visa) or to freelancer/student visas.

## Do I need an attested degree for my visa?

It depends on your job title/designation on the visa, not on your actual qualifications alone — see
`customer/designations_and_qualifications.md` for the full explanation and the exceptions (e.g. a
sole shareholder who is also the company's Manager typically does **not** need an attested degree).

## How many visas can our company sponsor?

This is tied to your company's visa allocation, which is set based on your license/facility type.
If you need more visas than your current allocation allows, GGBH can help submit a Visa Allocation
Amendment to request an increase — this is a normal, common step, not a sign of a problem with your
application.

## What if I have an overstay fine?

If there's an outstanding UAE overstay fine on your record, it needs to be paid before certain visa
actions (like a Change of Status) can proceed, and fines can increase daily if unpaid. Tell your
consultant right away if you think this might apply to you — they'll confirm the exact amount and
help you clear it quickly so it doesn't delay your application.

## Refunds

- If your visa application has already been submitted to UAE immigration (GDRFA), it generally
  **cannot be refunded**, regardless of the outcome, except in the specific case below.
- A refund **is** possible if GDRFA formally **rejects** the application (or it was never actually
  submitted) — even then, government fees, VAT, and processing fee components already incurred are
  deducted before any refund.
- If you change your mind after your application has already reached the submission stage, or if
  the applicant is blacklisted, a refund is not available.

## Common doubts

- **"Why do you need my bank statement?"** It's a UAE immigration requirement for Investor/Partner
  visas, used to confirm financial standing — not a GGBH-specific request.
- **"Can I switch visa designation later?"** Sometimes, but it's treated as a new application with
  its own document and eligibility check — not an automatic change.
- **"How long does the visa process take?"** Depends on visa type, nationality, and whether any
  extra approval or documentation is needed — your consultant will give you a specific estimate once
  your case is reviewed.
