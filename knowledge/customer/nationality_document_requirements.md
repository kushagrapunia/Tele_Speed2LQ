# Nationality-specific document requirements — customer FAQ (GGBH)

**Audience:** customers asking "what documents do I need for my nationality?" Beyond the standard
passport copy + photo, UAE immigration asks for extra documents for some nationalities. This file
lists the most common ones GGBH has seen; if a customer's nationality isn't listed, or their
document doesn't match what's described, tell them a GGBH consultant will confirm the exact
requirement rather than guessing.

## GCC nationals (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain)

No residence visa required at all — see `customer/visa_and_immigration.md`. Saudi applicants
additionally provide the passport's full signature page spread.

## Extra document commonly required: National ID (front & back)

Applicants from **Afghanistan, Iran, Pakistan, Iraq** are generally asked for a National ID
(front and back) alongside the passport. For Pakistan specifically, double-check your National ID
hasn't expired before submitting.

## Extra document commonly required: father's-name / additional-name page

Applicants from **India, Indonesia, Sri Lanka** are generally asked for a specific extra passport
page (varies by country) that shows additional name information UAE immigration requires.

## Police Clearance Certificate

Applicants from **Nepal (employment visas only — family visit/residency visas are exempt), Kenya,
Uganda, Senegal, Nigeria, Cameroon** are generally required to provide a Police Clearance / Good
Conduct Certificate, attested as required (from your home country via the UAE embassy, or from
Dubai Police if you're already inside the UAE on a previous residency). Your consultant will confirm
the exact attestation route for your situation.

## Longer bank statement requirement (Investor/Partner visas)

Most nationalities need a 3-month bank statement (see `customer/visa_and_immigration.md`), but these
nationalities are generally asked for **6 months** instead:

- Kenya
- Nigeria (also requires a valid credit card, front & back)
- Cameroon (credit card required — must be credit, not debit)

## Passport type restrictions

- **Latvia, Lithuania, Turkey:** the passport cover page must be a standard (typically red) civilian
  passport — official/diplomatic passport types are not accepted for this process.
- **"Provisional" passport type** is not accepted for any nationality.
- **"Aliens" passport type** is no longer accepted by UAE immigration for any nationality.

## Pre-medical documentation

Applicants from **Ethiopia, Sri Lanka, Indonesia**, and in some cases **Egypt**, are generally
required to complete a pre-medical clearance step before or alongside their visa application. Your
consultant will confirm whether this applies to you.

## Bangladesh — outside-country restriction

If you're applying **from outside the UAE** and hold Bangladeshi nationality:

- **Investor visa** is available to apply for from outside the UAE.
- **Partner visa** is **not** available from outside — it must be applied for from inside the UAE.
- For other visa designations, only a specific pre-approved subset can be applied for from outside
  the UAE. **GGBH will confirm your specific eligibility before starting an outside-the-country
  application for any other designation** — this list is verified case by case rather than assumed.

If you're applying from **inside** the UAE, this restriction does not apply.

## Special passport cases (two countries sharing a name)

A few country names are shared by two distinct nations with different document handling —
if either of these applies to you, mention it explicitly so your consultant processes it correctly:

- **Somaliland vs. Somalia** — different countries, handled differently.
- **Congo (Democratic Republic) vs. Congo (Republic)** — different countries, different
  nationality labelling.
- **Dominica (Commonwealth) vs. Dominican Republic** — different countries.

## Common doubts

- **"Why do you need an extra document just for my nationality?"** These are UAE immigration
  requirements tied to your nationality, not something GGBH adds on top — they vary by country and
  by visa type.
- **"My nationality isn't listed here — do I need anything extra?"** Possibly not, but your GGBH
  consultant will confirm based on your specific passport and visa type.
