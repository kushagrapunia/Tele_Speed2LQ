# Designations & qualifications — customer FAQ (GGBH)

**Audience:** customers asking "does my job title need a degree?" or "will my qualification be
accepted?" Answer using the general rule below; where GGBH's own data has a known gap (flagged
below), say so honestly rather than guessing at a specific designation.

## Does my visa designation need an attested degree?

The rule depends on your role, not just your personal qualifications:

1. **If you're the sole shareholder of the company and also named as Manager on the trade
   license**, you can generally apply under an Investor or General Manager designation **without**
   needing an attested degree.
2. **If you're already named as Manager on the trade license**, you don't need an attested degree
   to hold that same managerial designation on your visa.
3. **For most other managerial, directorial, financial, and similar senior designations**, UAE
   immigration requires a **degree attested by both your home country's relevant authority and the
   UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA)**. A plausible-looking but unattested certificate will not
   be accepted — both attestation stamps are required.
4. **Most non-managerial, technical, and operational job titles** generally do not require an
   attested degree — but if you're unsure whether your specific title counts as "managerial," ask
   your consultant rather than assuming either way.
5. If you're applying for a managerial title (e.g. General Manager) but you are **not** actually
   named as Manager on the license, rule 2 does not apply to you — rule 3's attestation requirement
   does.

## ⚠️ A small number of designations are still being confirmed

GGBH's internal designation data includes a set of specialized job titles (things like Compliance
Executive, Logistics Coordinator, Site Engineer, and similar non-standard titles) where whether an
attested degree is required has **not yet been confirmed** with full certainty. If a customer's
intended designation falls into this less-common category, be upfront: *"For that specific
designation, our team is finalizing the degree requirement — we'll confirm it with you directly
before you apply, rather than assume either way."* Do not tell a customer they definitely do or
don't need a degree for one of these titles.

## Will my degree/qualification be accepted?

UAE immigration matches your degree or certificate title against its own approved list of
qualifications (not any degree title you provide informally). In practice:

- Your degree needs to be from a recognized university or college — not a training certificate or
  short course.
- It must carry the two-part attestation described above (home country + UAE MOFA).
- Your consultant will match your exact degree title (e.g. "Bachelor of Business Administration,"
  "B.Sc. Civil Engineering") against the approved list. If your degree title is unusual or doesn't
  match cleanly, don't worry — your consultant will help identify the correct match rather than
  leaving it to guesswork, since an incorrect match can cause a rejection.

## Common doubts

- **"I have a degree, why do I still need attestation?"** Attestation confirms the degree is
  genuine and recognized by both your home country and the UAE — it's a separate step from simply
  holding the degree.
- **"My job title sounds managerial but I'm not sure it counts."** That's exactly the kind of case
  to bring to a consultant rather than guess — the distinction matters for whether a degree is
  required.
- **"Can I apply for a designation I'm not confident about, and change it later?"** Generally no —
  it's better to confirm the correct designation and its degree requirement before applying, since
  changing it afterward is treated as a new application.
