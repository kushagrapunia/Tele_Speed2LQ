# Business setup & activities — customer FAQ (GGBH)

**Audience:** prospective customers asking about setting up a business with Gulf Gateway Business
Hub (GGBH). Answer in plain language; never invent a specific fee, timeline, or approval that isn't
stated here — point the customer to a free consultation for anything case-specific.

## What GGBH does

GGBH helps you set up and license a business in a UAE free zone, and handles the visa/immigration
process for you and your team once the company is formed.

## What business activities can I do?

UAE free zone licenses are generally grouped into a few categories:

- **Commercial** — trading, import/export, general buying and selling of goods.
- **Professional / Service** — consultancy, IT services, marketing, media, and other service-based
  work.
- **Industrial** — light manufacturing, assembly, or production activities.
- **Freelance / Individual permits** — for a single professional operating under their own name,
  where the free zone offers this option.

Most free zones allow a company to combine several related activities under one license. Your GGBH
consultant will confirm exactly which activities are available on your chosen license and which
combinations are allowed — this varies by free zone and changes from time to time, so don't rely on
a general answer for your specific case.

## What needs extra approval before I can get my license?

Most standard commercial and professional activities only need the free zone's own license — no
extra government approval. A smaller set of **regulated activities** need a No Objection Certificate
(NOC) or a separate license/approval from another government authority *before* the trade license
can be issued. Common examples of activities that usually need this extra step:

- Legal consultancy / legal services
- Medical, healthcare, or clinical services
- Education, training centres, and tutoring
- Media, publishing, and broadcasting
- Financial services, insurance, and investment advisory
- Engineering, construction, and industrial safety
- Security services and security equipment

**If your activity falls into one of these categories, expect an extra approval step and a longer
timeline** — GGBH manages this NOC/approval process on your behalf, but the requesting authority
(not GGBH) sets the pace and requirements. If you're not sure whether your activity needs this,
ask your GGBH consultant to check the exact activity code before you commit to a timeline.

## What's the cost and timeline?

These depend on your business activity, license type, facility/office requirement, and the number
of visas you need — there is no single fixed number that applies to everyone. A free consultation
with GGBH will confirm the exact cost and timeline for your specific situation.

## What's the general process?

1. Choose your business activity and license type (with GGBH's guidance).
2. Confirm the shareholding structure (sole owner, partners, or corporate shareholder).
3. Apply for the trade license — GGBH prepares and submits this for you, including any regulated-
   activity NOC where required.
4. Once licensed, GGBH sets up your company's Establishment Card, which is what allows your company
   to sponsor visas.
5. From there, visa applications for you and any employees/partners can begin — see
   `customer/visa_and_immigration.md` for what's involved.

## What do I need to provide to get started?

At minimum: your passport copy, a general description of your intended business activity, and the
proposed shareholding structure. Everything else (documents for specific visa types, nationality-
specific paperwork) is confirmed once your activity and structure are set — see
`customer/nationality_document_requirements.md` and `customer/visa_and_immigration.md`.

## Common doubts

- **"Can I add more activities later?"** Usually yes, subject to the free zone's rules and possibly
  an amendment fee — confirm with your consultant.
- **"Can I change my license type after setup?"** Often possible, but treated as an amendment with
  its own process — not something to assume is free or instant.
- **"Do I need a physical office?"** Depends on the free zone and license type; some packages
  include a flexi-desk or virtual office option. Confirm with your consultant.
